# 宇宙的使命：我的个人经历与宣言 (Mission of the Universe: Personal Experience and Manifesto)

> **“我不是在研究科学，我是在翻译一种超前的文明。”**

### 1. 卑微的起点 (Humble Beginnings)

* **[中]** 我出生在中国安徽省庐江县的一个泥土房子里。那是家里的厨房，地上铺满了枯草，我就在那样的环境中来到了这个世界。我的一生注定与土地和底层社会紧密相连。
* **[EN]** I was born in a mud house in Lujiang County, Anhui Province, China. It was in the family kitchen, on a floor covered with dry grass. My life was destined to be deeply connected with the earth and the bottom of society.

---

### 2. 1985年的奇遇与预言 (The 1985 Encounter and Prophecy)

* **[中]** 1985年，我在芜湖遇到了一位改变我命运的老者。他预言我是一个不简单的人，未来会影响整个人类的历史，甚至被称为“圣人”。
* **[EN]** In 1985, I met an elder in Wuhu who changed my destiny. He prophesied that I was no ordinary man and that I would one day influence the entire history of humanity, even being called a "Saint."
* **[中]** 老者告诉我，我会毁誉参半，生活在社会最底层，为生存拼命。但他指引我：必须刻苦学习现代科学基础知识，才能用人类的语言将“神人”（外星人）的科学讲清楚，从而被大众接受。
* **[EN]** The elder told me I would be both praised and slandered, living at the lowest level of society, struggling for survival. However, he guided me: I must study basic modern science diligently to translate "extraterrestrial" science into human language so it could be accepted by the public.

---

### 3. 三十余年的孤独探索 (Decades of Solitary Exploration)

* **[中]** 几十年来，我一直坚守在地下室或简陋的实验室里。尽管面临贫穷、黑粉的谩骂以及账号被封禁的困扰，我从未放弃过对《统一场论》的完善和实验。
* **[EN]** For decades, I have persisted in basements and humble laboratories. Despite poverty, harassment from "black fans," and social media bans, I have never given up on refining the "Unified Field Theory" and its experiments.
* **[中]** 我之所以在2025年坚持在美国申请专利（专利号：12417870），是因为在国内申请时受到了大量的恶意举报。但我坚信，真理是不受国界限制的。
* **[EN]** The reason I insisted on applying for a patent in the U.S. in 2025 (No. 12417870) was because I faced numerous malicious reports while applying in China. I firmly believe that truth knows no borders.

---

### 4. 科学创新的终极目标 (Final Goal of Scientific Innovation)

* **[中]** 我的创新理论不是为了名利，而是为了让人类摆脱对化石能源的依赖。通过人工场扫描技术，人类可以实现瞬间移动、光速飞行，甚至是意识的数字化延续。
* **[EN]** My innovative theory is not for fame or fortune, but to free humanity from its dependence on fossil fuels. Through Artificial Field Scanning (AFS) technology, humans can achieve teleportation, light-speed flight, and even the digital continuation of consciousness.
* **[中]** 我鄙视无益的言论，我坚信只有技术创新能为人类带来真正的幸福。
* **[EN]** I despise useless talk and firmly believe that only technological innovation can bring true happiness to humanity.
